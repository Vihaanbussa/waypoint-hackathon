  1. 2:00 AM

    The Manager starts the night

    One Bot is in charge and holds the only schedule. It wakes Case Clerk and Scout, waits for both, and asks once more if one fails before going on with the last good data.

  2. 2:01 AM

    Case Clerk reads the government portal

    It signs in, looks up your receipt number and reads the status word for word. It only reads. It never submits or uploads anything.

  3. 2:05 AM

    Scout watches X and reads your inbox

    News about visas breaks on X first, so it searches X for your visa type and checks each claim against USCIS and the Federal Register. A post only counts once an official page confirms it. Then it finds dates in mail from USCIS, your school and your employer, and never replies, forwards or deletes.

  4. 2:30 AM

    The Manager counts the days and drafts

    Plain code does the date math from the official rule, so the number is the same every time. The Manager drafts the email someone else needs to get, then holds it. Nothing goes to another person until you approve.

  5. 2:40 AM

    Messenger puts it where you will see it

    One calendar event per deadline, and one text if something needs you now. Those go only to you, so they need no approval. It keeps to your quiet hours unless it is urgent.

  6. 8:00 AM

    You open one page

    Everything is sorted by how soon it needs you. You read the top deadline, approve the one email the Manager held for you, and you are done.

The fourth worker, Intake Clerk, reads a document whenever you send it one.

Three things Waypoint never does

  • It never lets AI do the date math.

    Days left come from plain code and the official rule. The number is the same every time you look.

  • It never contacts anyone for you on its own.

    A Bot can only draft. Nothing reaches your employer, school or attorney until you approve it.

  • It never puts your ID numbers in a message.

    A text or email names the deadline and the days left. Receipt numbers and documents stay out of it.
